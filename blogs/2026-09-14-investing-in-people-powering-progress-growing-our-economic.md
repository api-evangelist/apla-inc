Rapid technological and economic shifts continue to widen the gap in access to stable careers, particularly in underserved communities. Closing that gap has to be an intentional effort, not an accident. Since 2016, Aplahub’s Prosperity Hubs have taken on that challenge with a simple belief: talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not.
