Load tests can be automated. Completing the analysis and reflecting the findings into HPA and CPU/memory requests is the part that usually happens once at launch and then rarely again. IKS AIR fixes that with AIR Managed Autoscaling: it sizes each service continuously from real traffic instead of a guess.
